Why MLS Exists

Looking beyond right and wrong answers

MLS seeks to understand what the learner knows, what strategy was used, where difficulty begins, and what support may help next.

Review

Examine worksheets, tests, quizzes, assignments, and workbook pages.

Understand

Identify demonstrated skills, strategies, recurring errors, and possible learning gaps.

Respond

Select a focused next learning activity based on available evidence.

For Families

Keep your current curriculum.

MLS can begin by reviewing mathematics work your learner is already completing. No curriculum replacement is required.

  • Worksheets and workbook pages
  • Tests and quizzes
  • Assignments and projects
  • Parent observations
