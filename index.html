Math Logic Systems

Understanding how people learn mathematics.

Math Logic Systems (MLS) is being developed to help identify what learners know, where difficulties begin, and what learning experiences may help next.

Why MLS Exists

Many mathematics programs focus primarily on whether an answer is correct or incorrect. MLS seeks to understand what a learner knows, where difficulty begins, and what support may help next.
